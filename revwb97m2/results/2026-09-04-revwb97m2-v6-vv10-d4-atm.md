## Decision

The active revised model is `R2_coachform_292`. It preserves the 288 COACH-form
semilocal columns and appends four scalar energy features:

| Index | Feature | Fitted coefficient |
|---:|---|---|
| 288 | unscaled short-range HF exchange | `c_sr_hf` |
| 289 | VV10 correlation (`b=10`, `C=0.01`) | `c_vv10` |
| 290 | total frozen-core canonical RI-MP2 correlation | `c_pt2` |
| 291 | COACH pure three-body D4-ATM | `c_d4_atm` |

The D4-ATM feature freezes `s6=0`, `s8=0`, `s9=1`, `a1=0.215`, `a2=5.8`,
and `alp=16`, matching `coach/FunctionalCOACH/coach_pyscf.py`. The internal
`s9=1` defines the raw ATM energy; it is not the revwb97m2 fitted coefficient.

The original/published-model equality `c_vv10 + c_pt2 = 1` is removed from the
revised model. `c_vv10`, `c_pt2`, and `c_d4_atm` are independent fitted
coefficients with conceptual bounds `(0,1)`. The solver representation uses
the closed interior approximation `[1e-8, 0.99999999]` because strict
inequalities are not representable in a mathematical programming model.

The immutable published omegaB97M(2) R0 comparator retains its historical
coefficients and identities; this amendment changes only the revised fit.
